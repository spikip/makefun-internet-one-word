## About this entry

The word is "cookies", and it earns its place three ways at once.
First, it is a joke that needs no setup: every site on the internet
opens by begging you to accept its cookies, so the word already makes
people smile before they have thought about it. Second, it is true
about the whole internet rather than one corner of it, because the
tracking cookie is the thing the entire ad economy runs on, from the
smallest blog to the largest platform. Third, it is a surprise: the
obvious answers are words like connected or endless, while cookies
sneaks in sideways and turns out to fit better than either of them.
It is seven letters, correctly spelled, instantly readable, and it
describes the internet as a place that feeds you something sweet
while quietly keeping a record of everything you did there. The
program holds the word, checks it against the bounty's gate, and
writes it to /output/entry.txt exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one word to `/output/entry.txt`.
